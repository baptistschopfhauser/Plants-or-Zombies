Bloomguard Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Bloomguard is a single-player offline game. This policy explains what the game does with data. In short: nothing leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. Bloomguard does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your level progress, stars, unlocked guardians and settings in the app's local storage on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data is never uploaded. Uninstalling the app or choosing Settings → Reset progress deletes it.

Permissions

The Android app declares the standard Internet permission used by its web view runtime. The game does not make network requests.

Children

Bloomguard is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
